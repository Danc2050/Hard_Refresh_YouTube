YouTube Night Block — Options

Tabs to reset When the block turns on, tabs matching new RegExp(pattern, flags) are reloaded, which sends YouTube pages to the bedtime page at http://192.168.68.67:8080/. Everything else is left alone.
Schedule
Windows may wrap past midnight (e.g. 22:00 → 06:00). Blocking itself is enforced by the extension's rules, so it does not depend on DNS.
Manual
Forces the block on so you can test it. If you are outside the scheduled window, it reverts within about 5 minutes.
